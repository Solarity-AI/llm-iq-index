Method

How a score is made

  1. Benchmark mapping. Each thinking mode's Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index (v5, 0–100) converts to IQ with IQ = 100 + 0.6 × index. The factor puts the strongest modes where TrackingAI's offline test measures them, around 130–135.
  2. Direct IQ test. Where TrackingAI tested that exact model and mode on its offline test, whose questions are never published, the final score averages the mapped number with the measured IQ. These rows carry a tested mark; hover it for the raw results.
  3. Why not Mensa Norway. Top models nearly max out the public Mensa Norway test (147–148) and may have seen its questions in training. It appears only in tooltips.
  4. Read with care. These are estimates of reasoning ability on an IQ-style scale, not psychometric IQ. Treat gaps of 2–3 points as noise. Kimi K3.1 Pro weights were announced but not yet released. Gemini 3.8 Flash's tested mode is matched to "high" as the closest fit.
